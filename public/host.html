How to play

📱
1 · JoinEveryone scans the QR code on the TV with their phone. No app needed. 4+ players is best.
✨
2 · SculptOn your turn, pick a word. Hold your phone up like a camera, hold the button and sweep through the air to paint. Twist your wrist to push the ink farther away.
🌀
3 · SpinThe TV shows the sculpture from a weird angle that slowly swings to the front. On your phone, drag to orbit it and pinch to zoom.
📳
4 · Shake & guessShake your phone to unlock a guess. A wrong guess freezes you for 4 seconds. The first correct guess ends the turn.
⚡
5 · Power-upsStart with one, earn more by guessing right, and grab power orbs when they drop. Pick a rival and flick your phone to throw it:
🌫️ Fog their view🙃 Flip it upside down🌀 Make them dizzy🧊 Freeze their guessing💡 Secret letter for you🛡️ Shield yourself
🤖
Bonus · Beat the AIGemini plays along: three times a turn it looks at the sculpture and takes a guess. If it gets there first, it steals the points. Throw power-ups at it to blind it! After each turn it reviews the sculpture out of 10.
🏆
6 · ScoringThe guesser scores 100–500 points (faster = more), and the sculptor gets half of that. Hard words are worth ×1.5. Most points after the last round wins.
